Tools that support your goals, not the other way around.

Over the years, we have worked with dozens of data technologies and continue to adopt new ones. For us, the tools and technology are never the starting point. The starting point is the client’s business challenge. We identify the solution that best fits your architecture, requirements and business logic, and select the technologies accordingly. At the same time, certain tools have consistently proven their value across projects, including Tableau, Snowflake and dbt. We have therefore established official partnerships with these providers and use their technologies extensively.

Start with the problem, not the tool

One of the most common mistakes in data projects is choosing a technology before truly understanding the problem. We see this regularly: an organisation may have purchased several terabytes of Vertica capacity, only to find its actual data volumes and user base fall well short of that capacity. The result is over-investment and unnecessarily high fixed costs. In another case, Tableau has been adopted, but the underlying data remains fragmented and the tool ends up being used mainly as an ETL workaround or to replace old Excel reports. In that case, the underlying problem isn’t solved: a new tool is simply added on top of the existing complexity.

We take the opposite approach: we start by analysing the situation and understanding the problem, and only then recommend the right solution, of which technology is just one part. Sometimes that’s Snowflake, sometimes PostgreSQL. Sometimes what’s needed isn’t a new tool at all, just better-quality data on the existing platform.

Being a certified partner doesn’t change that principle. It gives us deeper expertise in the technologies that most often prove to be the right fit for businesses.
